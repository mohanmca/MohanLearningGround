### The most important ones to memorize first

For an OMS/FIX interview or day-to-day debugging, I would start with this chain:

**11 → Tot → ClOrdID**
**37 → Mug → OrderID**
**38 → Movie → OrderQty**
**39 → Mop → OrdStatus**
**40 → Rose → OrdType**
**44 → Roar → Price**
**54 → Lure → Side**
**55 → Lily → Symbol**
**59 → Lip → TimeInForce**
**60 → Cheese → TransactTime**
**150 → Dallas → ExecType**
**151 → Toilet → LeavesQty**

You can even visualize one ridiculous story:

**A TOT (11)** sends an order, gets a **MUG (37)** as its OrderID, buys **MOVIE (38)** tickets, cleans them with a **MOP (39)**, gives a **ROSE (40)** order type, **ROARS (44)** at the price, uses a **LURE (54)** for Buy/Sell, picks a **LILY (55)** symbol, shouts DAY/IOC with his **LIP (59)**, checks the time while eating **CHEESE (60)**, executes in **DALLAS (150)**, and leaves the remainder in the **TOILET (151)**.

The more absurd the image, the easier Lorayne's method works.
